The Vastu Mandala Compass

Vastu Shastra is the ancient scientific design system of spatial placement and solar alignment. Click any directional point on our interactive compass below to inspect room configurations, cosmic elements, and architectural science.

VASTU
KUBERA - NORTH

Wealth & Prosperity (North)

Element: Air (Vayu)

Recommended Placements

  • Main Entrance / Living Room
  • Treasury / Safe Room
  • Study Room / Library

Placements to Strictly Avoid

  • Master Bedroom
  • Kitchen (Fire element clashes)
  • Toilet / Septic Tank

Scientific Architectural Rationale

The North direction is associated with magnetic polar forces and receives cool, indirect, and constant light throughout the day. Placements of studies and living rooms here optimize daylight comfort without introducing excessive solar heat, while placing heavy services here blocks healthy ventilation flows.
